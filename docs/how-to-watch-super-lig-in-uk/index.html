How to watch the Süper Lig in the UK

Every legal option for 2026/27, Turkish clubs in Europe, the national team and UK kick-off times.

Short answer: the Süper Lig is not on any UK TV channel in 2026/27. The legal way to watch every match live in the UK is Digiturk Play's international Sports package, an internet streaming service from Digiturk, the beIN Media Group company that holds the league's rights.

1. Is the Süper Lig on Sky, TNT or any UK channel?

No. Sky Sports, TNT Sports, Premier Sports, the BBC, ITV and the UK streaming services do not have the 2026/27 Süper Lig rights, so you will not find Galatasaray, Fenerbahçe, Beşiktaş, Trabzonspor or any other Süper Lig match in UK TV listings. That is why the Süper Lig page on Maç Saati shows UK kick-off times with the legal UK streaming option (Digiturk Play) and the Turkish broadcasters, rather than a UK TV channel.

2. The legal option: Digiturk Play abroad (Yurt Dışı)

Digiturk sells an international version of its streaming service for people living outside Türkiye. With the Sports package you get:

  • every Trendyol Süper Lig and 1. Lig match live on the beIN SPORTS channels, with Turkish commentary;
  • the Turkish national channels (TRT 1, ATV, Kanal D, Show TV and others) that also come with the cheaper Family package;
  • apps for smart TVs, Amazon Fire TV, Apple TV, phones, tablets and web browsers. No satellite dish is needed.

Important: the Family package on its own does not include the Süper Lig. You need the Sports package (sold as "Sports + Family").

Price: plans are prepaid for 3, 6, 12 or 24 months. In November 2026, UK sellers were advertising the 12-month Sports package at around £119. Prices change and vary by seller, so check before you buy, and buy from Digiturk or an authorised seller. The official app is "Digiturk Play Yurt Dışı" by DIGITURK.

3. What about TOD, beIN CONNECT or a VPN?

TOD and beIN CONNECT subscriptions bought in Türkiye are meant for viewing in Türkiye. Their terms restrict use from abroad, and using a VPN to get round that breaks the terms and can get your account closed. Maç Saati lists them only so you know which Turkish channel shows a match. Unofficial "IPTV" boxes and free streaming sites are illegal in the UK.

4. Turkish clubs in Europe are on UK TV

When Galatasaray, Fenerbahçe, Beşiktaş, Trabzonspor or another Turkish club plays in Europe, the match is on UK TV:

  • Champions League: TNT Sports and HBO Max show most matches; Prime Video shows one top match every Tuesday.
  • Europa League and Conference League: TNT Sports and HBO Max.

Every one of these is in the full listings, highlighted in red.

5. Türkiye national team

Türkiye's Nations League matches are on Prime Video in the UK (and on ATV and S Sport in Türkiye). See the Türkiye on TV page for the next match.

6. UK kick-off times

Turkish time (TSİ) is 3 hours ahead of the UK from late October to late March and 2 hours ahead in summer. So a 20:00 TSİ match starts at 17:00 UK time in winter. Every match on Maç Saati shows the UK time first; tap TSİ (TR) at the top to switch.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Süper Lig on UK TV in 2026/27?

No. No UK channel such as Sky Sports, TNT Sports, Premier Sports, BBC or ITV has the rights to show Süper Lig matches in the 2026/27 season.

How can I watch the Süper Lig legally in the UK?

Through Digiturk Play's international service (Digiturk Play Yurt Dışı) with the Sports package. It streams every Süper Lig match live with Turkish commentary over the internet, on a smart TV, Fire TV stick, Apple TV, phone or computer. The Family package alone does not include the matches.

Can I use a TOD or beIN CONNECT account from Türkiye in the UK?

Those subscriptions are sold for viewing in Türkiye. Their terms restrict use abroad, and using a VPN to get round that breaks the terms and can get the account closed.

Where can I watch Galatasaray, Fenerbahçe or other Turkish clubs in Europe?

Champions League, Europa League and Conference League matches are on UK TV: TNT Sports and HBO Max show most of them, and Prime Video shows one top Tuesday Champions League match each week.

What time do Süper Lig matches kick off in the UK?

Turkish time (TSİ) is 3 hours ahead of the UK in winter and 2 hours ahead in summer. A 19:00 TSİ kick-off is 16:00 in the UK from late October to late March, and 17:00 the rest of the year.
